TL;DR: 提出一种无光刻的两步法：以热蒸发的自组装Ga纳米颗粒作为自消耗掩模，在Ar电感耦合等离子体反应离子刻蚀中直接构筑半导体纳米锥，适用于GaAs、GaP、Si及杂化钙钛矿。

Motivation: 半导体纳米结构是重要的光管理单元，可增强光-物质相互作用、抑制反射并提升光耦合；但其跨材料平台应用受限于光刻、多步图形转移、残留掩模和材料专用刻蚀工艺。

Method: 热蒸发自组装Ga纳米颗粒作为自消耗掩模，配合Ar电感耦合等离子体反应离子刻蚀（ICP-RIE）的两步无光刻策略；通过关联TEM（correlated TEM）解析GaAs上的形成机理。Ga纳米颗粒与暴露衬底的刻蚀速率相近，使Ga覆盖的台柱逐步转变为纯半导体纳米锥并保持结晶有序；纳米锥尺寸从数十至数百纳米，表面覆盖率可通过Ga沉积量与等离子体功率调控。

Result: 该工艺在GaP和Si上同样获得纳米锥，并在多晶杂化金属卤化物钙钛矿上形成致密纳米结构，未检测到PbI2生成。纳米结构化后，单晶衬底的太阳加权反射率最低为11–15%，钙钛矿在掩模完全消耗后达0.5%。

Conclusion: 该方法将自组装掩模、掩模消耗与图形转移集成于单一物理刻蚀步骤，为III-V族、Si及钙钛矿光电子器件提供了一条简便的光耦合表面制备路线。

Abstract: Semiconductor nanostructures are key light-management elements because their geometry can strengthen light-matter interaction, suppress reflection, and enhance optical coupling. Yet their adoption across material platforms is constrained by lithography, multistep pattern transfer, persistent masks, and material-specific etching. Here, we introduce a lithography-free two-step strategy in which thermally evaporated self-assembled Ga nanoparticles act as self-consuming masks during Ar inductively coupled plasma reactive-ion etching. Correlated transmission electron microscopy on GaAs reveals the formation mechanism. Comparable erosion of the Ga nanoparticles and exposed substrate progressively converts Ga-capped pedestals into pure semiconductor nanocones while preserving crystalline order. Nanocone dimensions extend from tens to hundreds of nanometres, and surface coverage is tunable through Ga deposition and plasma power. The same process produces nanocones on GaP and Si and dense nanostructures on polycrystalline hybrid metal-halide perovskite without detectable PbI2 formation. During nanostructuring, the sun-weighted reflectance reaches minima of 11-15% on the monocrystalline substrates, whereas the perovskite achieves 0.5% after complete mask consumption. The approach therefore combines a self-assembled mask, its consumption, and pattern transfer in a single physical etching step, providing a simple route to light-coupling surfaces for III-V, Si, and perovskite optoelectronics.
