Result: 仿真结果表明该存内计算系统可成功执行若干简单自然数运算，包括加倍（doubling）与加一（successor函数）；同时定性/定量讨论了优化几何、势垒、电流幅值与时序可提升鲁棒性与实用可行性。

Conclusion: 基于skyrmion的存内计算在原理与仿真层面可行，能够以磁轨道上的集体操控实现基本算术运算；但当前仅验证了简单运算，尚需在结构设计、势垒工程与驱动参数等方面进一步优化，以提升鲁棒性和实际可行性。

Abstract: We present a skyrmionic-based computation platform, able to perform fully in-memory computing by collectively manipulating the skyrmions that serve as bits of the binary memory. The computation protocols are implemented through driving currents and adequately located anisotropy defects that modify the dynamics of the skyrmions along a magnetic track. Simulations demonstrating the feasibility of such an in-memory computing system are shown for several basic and simple mathematical operations with natural numbers, such as doubling and adding one (the successor function). We also discuss how further optimization of the geometry, barrier profiles, current amplitudes, and timing parameters could improve both the robustness and the overall practical feasibility of the system.
