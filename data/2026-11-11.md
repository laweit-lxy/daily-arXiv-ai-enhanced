Motivation: 可见光与近紫外光在生物光子学、量子计算等应用中需要对光振幅和相位进行快速调控，但大多数可见光波段的集成光子平台缺乏GHz量级的调制能力，制约了可扩展的快速光控制。

Method: 在493 nm波长下构建薄膜铌酸锂（TFLN）平台，集成光栅耦合器、分束器、环形谐振器与调制器；采用分段行波电极结构的调制器设计，并通过环形谐振器测量传播损耗、表征平台的光折变响应。

Result: 分段行波调制器实现半波电压-长度积450 mV·cm、消光比>20 dB、带宽>50 GHz；环形谐振器测得传播损耗2.9 ± 0.2 dB/cm，并定量了光折变响应；该平台可用于面向离子阱系统的可见光敏捷光束传输。

Conclusion: 该工作展示了可见光波段TFLN平台具备GHz级高速调制能力与低损耗集成器件性能，可支撑快速可见光光束调控；但光折变效应与衬底均匀性仍是规模化扩展的主要挑战。

Abstract: Visible and near-ultraviolet light underpins applications from biophotonics to quantum computing, which demand fast control of optical amplitude and phase. Integrated photonics offers scalable control, yet most visible-wavelength platforms lack gigahertz-scale modulation. We demonstrate a thin-film lithium niobate platform at 493 nm integrating grating couplers, beamsplitters, ring resonators, and modulators. Segmented traveling-wave modulators achieve a half-wave voltage--length product of 450 mV$\cdot$cm, $>$20 dB extinction, and bandwidth beyond 50 GHz. Ring resonators show $2.9 \pm 0.2$ dB/cm propagation loss and quantify the platform's photorefractive response. This enables agile visible beam delivery for trapped-ion systems, although photorefraction and substrate uniformity remain key challenges to scaling.
